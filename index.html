Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Kutvio really free?

Yes. Kutvio is completely free — no watermark on your videos, no subscription, and no in-app purchases.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start editing. There is no sign-up and no login.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything — editing, previews, and exporting — is processed on your device. Your photos, videos, and audio never leave it.

Does it support Arabic text?

Yes. Arabic is a first-class language in Kutvio: text is shaped correctly, mixed Arabic and English works, and the app includes many Arabic fonts. The whole interface is available in Arabic with a right-to-left layout.
