Frequently asked questions

Everything you might want to know about Kutvio.

Is Kutvio really free?

Yes. Kutvio is completely free — no watermark on your videos, no subscription, and no in-app purchases.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start editing. There is no sign-up and no login.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything — editing, previews, and exporting — is processed on your device. Your photos, videos, and audio never leave it.

What export quality is supported?

You can export from 480p up to 4K, at 24 to 60 frames per second, in H.264 or the smaller HEVC format. The highest options depend on what your device's hardware supports.

Does it support Arabic text?

Yes. Arabic is a first-class language in Kutvio: text is shaped correctly, mixed Arabic and English works, and the app includes many Arabic fonts. The whole interface is available in Arabic with a right-to-left layout.

Does it work on iPad and tablets?

Yes. On iPad and Android tablets, Kutvio uses a dedicated layout with a tool rail, an always-visible inspector, and a larger timeline. It supports landscape, Split View and multitasking, and hardware keyboard shortcuts.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Kutvio works fully offline — no internet connection is needed to edit or export.

Why does the app ask for photo and microphone access?

Photo access lets you choose clips to edit and save your finished videos to your library. The microphone is used only when you record a voiceover. You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings.

How do I delete my data?

Delete projects inside the app, clear the cache in Settings, or uninstall the app. Nothing is stored on our servers.

How do I contact support?

Email us at support@kutvio.com or visit the Support page.
