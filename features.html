Everything you need to tell your story

Kutvio packs a full editing toolkit into a fast, touch-first app — free, offline, and without a watermark.

Timeline & clips

Precise, fast editing on a real multi-track timeline.

  • Split, trim & deleteCut at the playhead and drag handles to trim with frame accuracy.
  • Reorder & replaceLong-press to reorder clips; swap media while keeping all edits.
  • Magnetic snappingClips snap to the playhead, clip edges and beat markers, with haptics.
  • Undo & redoEvery change is undoable, and projects save automatically.

Speed

Slow down the moment or speed up the journey.

  • 0.1× to 100×Constant speed with an option to keep natural pitch or change it.
  • Speed curves Coming soonRamp presets like Montage and Bullet, plus custom curves.
  • Reverse Coming soonPlay any clip backwards.

Transform & canvas

Frame every shot for every platform.

  • Move, scale & rotatePinch and drag right on the canvas, with center snap guides.
  • Rotate & mirrorFix orientation in one tap.
  • Any aspect ratio9:16, 16:9, 1:1, 4:5, 4:3, 3:4, 21:9 or original.
  • BackgroundsSolid colors, a blurred copy of your clip, or your own image.

Advanced editing

Pro tools for more creative control.

  • Keyframes Coming soonAnimate position, scale, rotation and opacity over time.
  • Picture-in-picture & blending Coming soonLayer clips with opacity and blend modes.
  • Masks Coming soonShape masks with feathering and inversion.
  • Chroma key Coming soonRemove green or blue screens.

Visuals

Give every video its own look.

  • FiltersHand-tuned looks in Portrait, Life, Film, Cinematic, Vintage and B&W, with intensity control.
  • AdjustmentsBrightness, contrast, saturation, exposure, temperature, tint, highlights, shadows, sharpen, vignette and fade.
  • TransitionsFades, flashes, slides, wipes, zooms, circles and more, with adjustable duration.
  • Animations, effects & stickers Coming soonClip animations, visual effects and sticker packs.

Text

Titles and captions that look great in any language.

  • 30+ fontsIncluding 14 Arabic typefaces, all free for commercial use.
  • Full stylingColor, outline, shadow, background, spacing, line height and alignment.
  • Text animationsFade, slide, pop, zoom, typewriter, bounce, wave, blink and more.
  • TemplatesReady-made styles you can apply in one tap.
  • Perfect ArabicCorrect shaping and right-to-left text, identical in preview and export.

Audio

Sound that matches your story.

  • Music & sound effectsA royalty-free library, or import your own audio files.
  • VoiceoverRecord narration directly on the timeline.
  • Volume, fades & speedShape every audio clip, from 0% to 200% volume.
  • Voice effectsChipmunk, deep, robot, echo, megaphone, telephone and reverb.
  • Extract audio & beatsPull audio from any video and mark beats to cut on the rhythm.

Export

Share in stunning quality.

  • Up to 4K480p, 720p, 1080p, 2K or 4K at 24, 25, 30, 50 or 60 fps.
  • H.264 or HEVCChoose maximum compatibility or a smaller file.
  • Live size estimateKnow how big your video will be before you export.
  • Save & shareSave to your gallery, then share to TikTok, Instagram, WhatsApp and more.

Tablet & iPad

A layout made for bigger screens.

  • Studio layoutTool rail, a persistent inspector and a full-width timeline with track headers.
  • MultitaskingWorks in portrait, landscape, Split View, Slide Over and Stage Manager.
  • Keyboard shortcutsPlay, split, undo, redo, duplicate, step frames, zoom and export from the keyboard.
  • Pointer supportScroll to scrub and pinch to zoom on a trackpad or mouse.
