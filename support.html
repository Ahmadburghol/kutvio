Troubleshooting

Export fails or stops
  • Make sure your device has enough free storage — at least twice the estimated video size shown in the export sheet.
  • Try a lower resolution or frame rate (for example 1080p at 30 fps).
  • Keep Kutvio open with the screen on until the export finishes; the system may stop exports in the background.
  • Restart the app and try again.
Kutvio can't see my videos or photos
  • iPhone/iPad: open Settings → Kutvio → Photos and choose Full Access, or tap Manage in Kutvio's picker to add more items to Limited Access.
  • Android: open Settings → Apps → Kutvio → Permissions → Photos and videos, and allow access.
  • Videos stored only in iCloud may need to download first — make sure you're connected while importing.
Audio is out of sync
  • Update Kutvio to the latest version.
  • Remove the clip and import it again.
  • If the clip uses a very high or low speed, preview playback can be approximate — the exported video is always rendered in sync.

Reporting a bug

Including these details helps us fix problems quickly:

  • Device model (for example iPhone 15 Pro or Galaxy Tab S9)
  • Operating system version (iOS/iPadOS or Android)
  • Kutvio version (Settings → About)
  • Steps to reproduce the problem, and what you expected to happen
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if possible

We only use your email to reply to you. See our Privacy Policy.
